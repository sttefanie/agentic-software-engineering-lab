## Current State

No technology stack has been selected. The laboratory is technology-neutral during the bootstrap phase.

## Principles

When technology selections are made, they will follow these principles:

1. **Vendor Neutrality** — Avoid unnecessary lock-in to specific LLM providers or cloud vendors
2. **Explicitness** — Document why each technology was chosen
3. **Traceability** — Link technology choices to architectural decisions (ADRs)
4. **Flexibility** — Allow for alternative implementations or technology swaps where practical

## Next Steps

Technology stack decisions will be made as part of the architectural process in a subsequent project phase.
