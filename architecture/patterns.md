# Architectural Patterns and Design Patterns

**STATUS: NOT DEFINED**

This document will record architectural patterns and design patterns used in the Agentic Software Engineering Lab.

## Scope

When patterns are selected, this document will include:

- **Architectural Patterns** — Large-scale structural patterns (Layered, Hexagonal, Event-Driven, etc.)
- **Design Patterns** — Reusable solutions to common problems (Factory, Observer, Strategy, etc.)
- **Integration Patterns** — Patterns for multi-agent coordination and communication
- **Context Patterns** — Patterns specific to context engineering and routing
- **Evaluation Patterns** — Patterns for testing and validating agentic workflows

## Current State

No patterns have been selected or established. The laboratory is pattern-neutral during bootstrap.

## Principles

When patterns are adopted, they will:

1. Solve real problems in the laboratory (not adopted proactively)
2. Be explicitly documented with rationale
3. Be linked to architectural decisions
4. Support the research goals rather than constrain them

## Next Steps

Pattern selection will emerge from architectural decisions and early experimental implementations in subsequent project phases.
