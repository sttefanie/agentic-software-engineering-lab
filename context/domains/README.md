# Domain Context

**Domain Context** represents knowledge about business domains, problem spaces, and subject matter areas relevant to the software engineering tasks the laboratory will evaluate.

## Purpose

Domain context will include:

- Domain-specific terminology and concepts
- Business rules and constraints
- Domain requirements and workflows
- Industry standards and best practices
- Subject matter expert knowledge
- Use cases and scenarios

## Organization

Domain contexts will be organized by problem domain or subject area. Each domain may have:

- A domain specification document
- Terminology and glossary
- Business rules
- Relevant regulations or standards
- Example scenarios

## Current State

No domains have been defined yet. The laboratory is in the bootstrap phase.

## Next Steps

Domains will be identified and documented as the laboratory defines specific experimental scenarios and tasks.
